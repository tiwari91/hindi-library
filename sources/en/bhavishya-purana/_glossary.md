# Bhavishya Purana English glossary

Names in simple roman spelling, no diacritics.

- Shatanika: the king who listens (descendant of Ajamidha, Pandava line)
- Sumantu: the sage who tells the Purana; pupil of Vyasa
- Vyasa: son of Parashara and Satyavati
- Vaishampayana, Shaunaka, Jaimini, Paila, Yajnavalkya: sages
- Narayana, Hari, Madhava, Murari: names of Vishnu (keep as written)
- dharma: "duty" (or "right conduct" where the text means conduct); artha "wealth"; kama "desire"; moksha "liberation"
- varna: "class"; dvija: "twice-born"; viprah: "Brahmin"
- Purana "Purana", Bharata "Bharata" (the Mahabharata), Bhavishya "Bhavishya"
- "mahabaho": "mighty-armed one"; "rajendra": "best of kings"; "nripottama": "best of kings"
- Brackets [ ] only for a brief clarifying word.
